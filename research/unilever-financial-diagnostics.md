# Unilever: Financial Ratios, Peer Resilience and Disclosure Analysis

**Area:** Fundamental investment research / financial risk  
**Attribution:** Group Financial Data Analytics term paper, Fall 2025, by **Nataliia Amirkhanian, Taerang Bae, Athena Biju and Chi Nguyen**. Individual contribution to each component is not inferred from the group authorship.

## Research question

How should a consumer-goods company's profitability, liquidity, efficiency, leverage and management commentary be read together under changing macroeconomic conditions?

## Work included in the group report

- SEC EDGAR 10-K/20-F and XBRL-style financial data, with multi-year ratio and peer comparisons.
- Profitability, liquidity, inventory turnover, leverage and coverage measures.
- Annual-report language research using financial-domain sentiment lexicons, readability indicators and topic models.
- Stress scenarios connecting commodity costs, inflation, credit conditions, FX and supply-chain disruptions with balance-sheet capacity.
- A substantive distinction between **operating efficiency/profitability** and **short-term liquidity headroom**.

The group report's central argument is that strong efficiency and apparent operating stability need not imply ample liquidity buffers.

## Material analytical qualification

Related exploratory forecasting notebooks use contemporaneous changes in ROE together with lagged ROE to predict current ROE. Since `ROE_t = ROE_(t−1) + ΔROE_t`, such features leak the target into the predictors. Their near-perfect in-sample fit values should **not** be interpreted as defensible out-of-sample predictive performance.

Synthetic-data demonstrations elsewhere in the notebook family are not realised financial forecasts.

## Investment relevance

Useful as an illustration of reconciling accounting data, peer structure, credit resilience and textual management signals into a **risk question**. It is not an independently verified buy/sell recommendation, and its financial observations should be rechecked against company filings before professional use.
